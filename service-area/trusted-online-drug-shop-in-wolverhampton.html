Frequently asked questions

Is MidlandsRxUK a genuine, legal online pharmacy in the UK?

Yes. MidlandsRxUK is registered with the General Pharmaceutical Council (GPhC) and complies fully with UK medicines regulations. You can verify our registration on the official NHS online pharmacy checker. All medicines we dispense are sourced exclusively from licensed UK wholesalers.

Can I get prescription medicine delivered to my address in Wolverhampton?

Absolutely. We deliver to all Wolverhampton postcodes, including WV1, WV2, WV3, WV4, WV6, WV10, WV11, WV12, WV13, and WV14 and surrounding areas.

How do I transfer my NHS repeat prescription to MidlandsRxUK?

Transferring your NHS repeat prescription is simple. You can nominate MidlandsRxUK as your preferred pharmacy through the NHS app or by asking your GP surgery to update your nomination.

Are the medicines from MidlandsRxUK safe and genuine?

All medicines dispensed by MidlandsRxUK are 100% genuine and sourced only from MHRA-approved, licensed UK wholesalers.

What types of medication can I order online through MidlandsRxUK?

We offer a comprehensive range including NHS and private repeat prescriptions, over-the-counter medicines, and certain pharmacy-only treatments.
