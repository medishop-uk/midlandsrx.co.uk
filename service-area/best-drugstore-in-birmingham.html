FAQ About Our Online Drugstore

Do you always need a prescription to buy medicine from an online pharmacy?

For prescription-only medications, a valid prescription from a licensed healthcare professional is always required. Some online pharmacies provide an online doctor service, where their clinical team reviews a consultation to determine if a prescription is necessary.

Do you deliver to my area in Birmingham?

We deliver across all Birmingham postcodes and the wider West Midlands, including the city center, Edgbaston, Moseley, Sutton Coldfield, Handsworth, Solihull, Wolverhampton, and more. Orders placed before 3pm are typically dispatched the same day, with standard delivery arriving within 24–48 hours. All orders are sent in discreet, unmarked packaging.

How do I get a prescription from your online pharmacy?

Just fill out a short online questionnaire about your symptoms and medical history. A registered doctor will look over your answers and, if necessary, send a prescription straight to our pharmacy.

Can I use my existing NHS prescription with MidlandsRxUK?

Yes, we accept both NHS and private prescriptions. If you already have a valid NHS prescription from your GP, you can submit it to us for dispensing.

Are online dispensary prices cheaper than local pharmacies?
