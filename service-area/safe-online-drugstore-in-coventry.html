An online pharmacy lets you browse treatments, complete a consultation where needed, and place your order through a secure website. Once approved, your medicine is dispensed and delivered to your address.

That depends on the type of medicine. Some treatments require an online medical consultation and approval, while other products can be purchased without a prescription.
