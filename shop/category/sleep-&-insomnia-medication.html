FAQ about sleep & insomnia medicines

What are the purposes of sleep aids?

Sleep medicines are commonly used to help people who struggle with insomnia, difficulty falling asleep, or disturbed sleep patterns.

Is it possible to take medication for insomnia every night?

Some sleep medicines are meant for short-term use only, so it is important to follow proper medical guidance and dosage instructions.

Do sleep aids have negative side effects?

Yes, some users may experience dizziness, drowsiness, dry mouth, headache, or daytime sleepiness after taking sleep medication.

How long does it take for sleep aids to start working?

Most sleep medicines start working within 15 to 60 minutes depending on the type of medication and the person’s body response.

Can insomnia brought on by anxiety be treated with sleep aids?

Certain sleep medicines may also help calm anxiety and relax the mind, making it easier to fall asleep and stay asleep.
