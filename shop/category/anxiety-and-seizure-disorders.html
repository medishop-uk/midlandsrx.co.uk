FAQ about anxiety & seizure disorder medicines

What medicines are commonly used for anxiety disorders?

Doctors may prescribe medicines such as SSRIs, SNRIs, beta blockers, or short-term sedative medicines depending on the patient’s condition and medical history.

What medicines are used to treat seizure disorders?

Seizure disorders are usually treated with anti-seizure medicines, also known as anticonvulsants or antiepileptic drugs, which help control abnormal brain activity.

Can the same medicine treat anxiety and seizures?

Some medicines, such as certain benzodiazepines, may be used for both anxiety symptoms and seizure control, but only under strict medical supervision.

Are anxiety and seizure medicines safe for long-term use?

Safety depends on the medicine, dosage, and patient’s health condition; some medicines are suitable long-term, while others may cause dependence or withdrawal.

Can I stop anxiety or seizure medicine suddenly?

No, these medicines should not be stopped suddenly without medical advice because symptoms may return, worsen, or cause withdrawal effects.
