FAQ about anxiety and panic disorder medicines

What medicines are commonly used for anxiety and panic disorder?

Doctors may prescribe SSRIs, SNRIs, beta blockers, or short-term sedative medicines depending on symptoms, severity, and patient history.

How long do anxiety medicines take to work?

Some medicines may take a few weeks to show full effect, while short-term calming medicines may work faster when prescribed by a doctor.

Are panic disorder medicines safe for daily use?

Some medicines are suitable for daily use under medical supervision, but others may cause dependence if used regularly for a long time.

Can anxiety medicine stop panic attacks?

Certain prescribed medicines can help reduce the frequency and intensity of panic attacks, especially when combined with therapy and lifestyle support.

Can I stop taking anxiety or panic medicine suddenly?

No, many anxiety and panic medicines should not be stopped suddenly because symptoms may return or withdrawal effects may occur.
