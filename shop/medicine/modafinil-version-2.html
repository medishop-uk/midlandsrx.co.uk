Side effects of modafinil

Modafinil is generally well tolerated, but some people may experience side effects while taking it. Most side effects are mild and may improve as the body adjusts to the medicine.

  • Headache is one of the most commonly reported side effects.
  • Some people may experience nausea or an upset stomach.
  • Difficulty sleeping (insomnia) can occur, especially if taken late in the day.
  • Nervousness, anxiety, or feeling restless may develop in some users.
  • Dizziness or dry mouth can occasionally occur.

FAQ about modafinil

What is Modafinil used for?

Modafinil is used to promote wakefulness in people with conditions that cause excessive daytime sleepiness, such as narcolepsy and shift work sleep disorder.

How long does Modafinil take to work?

Most people start to feel the effects within 1 to 2 hours after taking a dose.

How long do the effects of Modafinil last?

The effects can last for around 10 to 15 hours, depending on the individual and dosage.

Can Modafinil help with studying?

Some people use Modafinil to improve focus and concentration, but it should only be taken when prescribed by a healthcare professional.

Does Modafinil cause sleep problems?

Yes, Modafinil may cause difficulty sleeping if taken too late in the day.

Is Modafinil addictive?

Modafinil has a lower risk of dependence than many stimulant medications, but it should still be used exactly as prescribed.
