Insomnia drug Zopiclone key features

Zopiclone is designed to help individuals manage severe sleep difficulties for a short period. Understanding its features and safety guidelines is important before use.

  • Effective sleep support: Helps people fall asleep faster and reduces night-time awakenings.
  • Recommended dosage: Most adults are prescribed 7.5 mg before bedtime, while elderly patients may start with 3.75 mg.
  • Pregnancy caution: Zopiclone should generally be avoided during pregnancy unless advised by a healthcare provider.
  • Breastfeeding safety: The medicine may pass into breast milk, so breastfeeding mothers should consult a doctor before use.
  • Short-term treatment: Usually prescribed for a limited period to avoid dependence or tolerance.

Side effect of sleep medicine

While zopiclone may help with temporary sleep issues, some people may experience negative side effects. Depending on the dosage, the patient's health, and how the medication is taken, these effects can change. Here are some side effects:

  • Drowsiness or sleepiness the next day
  • Bitter or metallic taste in the mouth
  • Dry mouth or throat discomfort
  • Dizziness, headache, or light-headed feeling
  • Confusion, memory problems, or unusual mood changes

FAQ about Zopiclone (7.5 mg/10 mg/20 mg)

What is Zopiclone's typical dosage?

While some patients may be prescribed 3.75 mg, the typical adult dosage is 7.5 mg taken before bed. Higher strengths should only be used as directed by a physician.

Does Zopiclone have adverse effects?

Indeed, headaches, dry mouth, dizziness, and a bitter taste are possible side effects. The following day, some people might feel drowsy, disoriented, or less awake.

How long is Zopiclone safe to take?

It is usually prescribed by doctors for a brief period of time, usually a few days to a few weeks. Long-term use may increase the risk of dependence, tolerance, or withdrawal symptoms.

Is 10 mg or 20 mg of zopiclone more potent than 7.5 mg?

Indeed, 10 mg and 20 mg are more potent than the standard dosage of 7.5 mg. Only under appropriate medical supervision should these higher dosages be administered.

Can Zopiclone be taken every night?

Long-term daily use of zopiclone is typically not advised. When prescribed by a physician, it is primarily used to treat insomnia temporarily.

Does Zopiclone cause you to feel drowsy the next day?

Yes, some people may feel drowsy, tired, dizzy, or less focused the next day. Avoid driving or using machinery if you still feel sleepy after taking it.
