Pregabalin (Pregacare NT)

★★★★★ Read or write a review

Pregacare-NT treats neuropathic pain caused by conditions like diabetic neuropathy and postherpetic neuralgia, using Mecobalamin, Pregabalin, and Nortriptyline to repair nerves and reduce pain signals. Common side effects include dizziness, sleepiness, nausea, blurred vision, and swelling, which usually improve over time.
