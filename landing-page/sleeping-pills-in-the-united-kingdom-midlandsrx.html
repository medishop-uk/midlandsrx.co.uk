How to check fake medicines

Fake medicines can be dangerous because they may contain the wrong ingredients, incorrect strength, or harmful substances. Before ordering any medicine online, customers should take a few simple safety steps.

  • Check whether the website has clear contact information.
  • Avoid sellers that offer strong medicines with no checks at all.
  • Look for proper product names, strength, packaging, and usage details.
  • Be careful with prices that look unrealistically cheap.
  • Avoid social media-only sellers with no reliable website or pharmacy information.
  • Do not buy medicines from unknown links or suspicious ads.
  • Ask questions before ordering if anything looks unclear.

FAQ about sleeping pills in the United Kingdom

Can I buy sleeping pills online in the UK?

Yes, some sleep-related medicines may be available online, but prescription-only sleeping pills should only be supplied after proper checks, valid prescription requirements, or clinical review. Always use sleeping pills responsibly and follow professional guidance.

What are sleeping pills used for?

Sleeping pills are usually used for short-term sleep problems, especially when insomnia affects daily life. They may help with falling asleep, staying asleep, or calming the body at night, depending on the medicine type.

Are sleeping pills safe for long-term use?

Many sleeping pills are not recommended for long-term use unless a qualified healthcare professional advises it. Long-term or incorrect use may increase the risk of dependence, tolerance, daytime drowsiness, and withdrawal problems.

What should I check before ordering sleeping pills online?

Check the website, product details, contact information, medicine strength, prescription requirements, and customer support process. Avoid any seller that offers strong sleeping tablets without asking proper safety or suitability questions.
