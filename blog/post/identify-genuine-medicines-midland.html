Guide to identify genuine medicines and avoid counterfeit drugs in the UK

Medicines are very crucial in reducing and treating illness, mitigating pain and giving support to patients for their recovery, and one of such reliable online pharmacies is MidlandsRx. However, not every medicine sold on online platforms or through any unofficial sellers are authentic. In the UK, availability of counterfeit drugs has become a great concern. These counterfeit or fake medicines may look identical, but they do not contain the same ingredients as the original ones. As a result, these counterfeit medicines may not provide effective treatment or lead people towards serious health hazards.

For the people living in the Midlands region in the UK in cities like Birmingham, Coventry, Leicester, Nottingham, Derby, Wolverhampton and others, MidlandsRx is a dependable online pharmacy for purchasing the original medicines.

What are counterfeit medicines

Counterfeit medicines are fake pharmaceutical products that do not contain the same or similar ingredients as the original medicines, and they are intentionally made to earn illegal profit. These counterfeit medicines are available on either online platforms or from unregulated sellers. They are mostly sold as branded products with limited options for checking or verification.

These counterfeit medicines may include:

·       Harmful ingredients

·       Incorrect ingredients

·       No active ingredients, etc.

MidlandsRx is following all the necessary safety standards for medicines to ensure that people are getting authentic and correct dosage for their ongoing treatments.

Why are counterfeit medicines dangerous?

Even though the counterfeit medicines look exactly like the original medicines, they can cause serious health issues. One of the primary risks associated with counterfeit medicines is that patients who take such fake medicines may inadvertently consume harmful ingredients that can increase health problems. Some other common risks associated with counterfeit medicines are –

·       Delay of existing treatment

·       treatment failure

·       unexpected side effects

·       consumption of toxic ingredients, etc.

People who are suffering from nerve pain or trying to manage their chronic conditions, consuming such counterfeit medicines can be very significant for then as it can degrade their health conditions.

 

How to identify genuine medicines in the UK?

Understanding the methods to check medicines before consuming them can be the best option for the people to avoid counterfeit medicines.

1.     Purchase medicines only from the licenced pharmacies

The first safe method is to purchase authentic medicines is to purchase them from licenced pharmacies who are registered under the General Pharmaceutical Council in the UK. These registered and licenced pharmacies will –

·       Display the licence and registration in a clear way

·       Provide authentic and valid UK address and information

·       Will always ask for prescription for prescription only medicines

2.     Check for the official UK Online Pharmacy Logo

For the online platforms there is MHRA Distance Selling Logo, and this logo ensures that the online pharmacy is legitimate. This logo ensures that this online seller is authorised to sell medicines on other online platforms throughout UK. However, it is important to understand that clicking on the logo should take the viewers to the official MHRA register to show that the online pharmacy is registered.

3.     Appearance competition between the genuine medicine and counterfeit ones

In authentic medicines, there should be physical characteristics that are consistent. For example, the shape of the product, it's colour and tablet markings should be the same for every other product.

If it does not appear to be the same as before then the person should speak to a professional pharmacist before consuming the product as it is possible that the manufacturers change the design or shape of the product.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How can I identify if the medicine is authentic or not in the UK?

You can get authentic and genuine medicines from pharmacies that are registered under General Pharmaceutical Council in the UK. For online platforms you can check that MHRA logo and verified packaging information like manufacturing date and expiry date, batch numbers, barcode, etc.

Is it safe to purchase medicine from online platforms in the UK?

Of course, if you purchase medicine products from a licensed and regulated online pharmacy, who asks for prescriptions from a UK registered doctor.

What should I do if I find counterfeit medicine?

If you find out or suspect the medicine is counterfeit, you should not consume the product further. After that you can report about the product to MHRA through the Yellow Card Scheme. You can also consult a nearby pharmacist.

Are counterfeit medicines available in the UK?

Yes, counterfeit medicines are available but not common in the UK as government regulations is very strict about it. These counterfeit medicines are often sold by online platforms and unverified marketplaces.

Why do counterfeit medicines look identical?

The primary purpose is to deceive the customers. That is why counterfeit medicines try to imitate the shape, colour, packaging, branding, and design of the original medicines.
